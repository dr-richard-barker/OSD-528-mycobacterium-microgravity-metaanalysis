\section{Results}

\subsection{Empirical Transcriptomic Divergence Across Microgravity Simulators}
Principal Component Analysis (PCA) calculated directly from real Illumina NextSeq 550 sequencing reads of NASA OSDR OSD-528 demonstrated clear biological and mechanical partitioning across the 9 biological samples (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig2}a). PC1 explained 70.1\% of the total transcriptomic variance, cleanly segregating the static 1g ground controls (RFPNG14, RFPNG35, RFPNG45) from simulated microgravity specimens. PC2 explained an additional 18.4\% of variance, resolving the mechanical simulation platforms (3D Clinostat vs. RPM 2.0).

Differential expression analysis on the real count matrix identified marked transcriptional remodeling across the three experimental contrasts (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig2}b):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{3D Clinostat vs. Static 1g}: Identified 351 significant DEGs (FDR $< 0.05$, $|\log_2\text{FC}| \ge 0.75$). Upregulated transcripts prominently featured uncharacterized proteins MMAR\_RS06635 ($\log_2\text{FC} = +6.29, \text{FDR} = 7.1 \times 10^{-14}$) and MMAR\_RS09245 ($+6.10$), FAD-binding oxidoreductase MMAR\_RS04125 ($+5.77$), amidase MMAR\_RS04845 ($+5.77$), cytidine deaminase MMAR\_RS05960 ($+5.44$), and NADH dehydrogenase subunit D \textit{nuoD} ($+2.51$). Downregulated transcripts included 50S ribosomal protein L33 \textit{rpmG} ($-7.31$), ESX-1 secretion-associated protein \textit{espB} ($-6.01$), alpha/beta fold hydrolase MMAR\_RS06010 ($-5.62$), and fatty acid CoA ligase \textit{fadD7} ($-5.29$).
    \item \textbf{RPM 2.0 vs. Static 1g}: Identified 738 significant DEGs, confirming the broader transcriptional perturbation of random multi-axis acceleration. Key induced transcripts included MarR-family transcriptional regulator MMAR\_RS04440 ($+5.76$) and NAD(P)H-binding protein MMAR\_RS21560 ($+4.88$), with cytochrome $bd$ oxidase \textit{cydA} ($+2.38$), while repressing tocopherol cyclase MMAR\_RS04050 ($-7.60$) and fatty acid ligase \textit{fadD7} ($-5.29$).
    \item \textbf{3D Clinostat vs. RPM 2.0}: Directly comparing the two simulators identified 242 significant DEGs, confirming that $>75\%$ of the transcriptomic alterations are shared and invariant to the mechanical simulation method, concordant with the biological observations of Clary et al. \cite{clary2022development}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Empirical Co-Expression Modules with GOSlim Functional Annotations}
Topological Overlap Matrix (TOM) clustering ($\beta=6$) over the 350 most variable transcriptomic features identified five coherent co-expression modules, which we annotated using standardized GOSlim functional definitions (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig3}a):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Cell Surface \& Biofilm Organization (\texttt{MEturquoise}, 168 genes)}: Captured the primary microgravity response core, enriched for cell wall assembly and membrane transport. Intramodular hub connectivity reached $k_{\text{within}} = 35.3$ for Zn-ribbon OB-fold protein MMAR\_RS12120, $35.0$ for NAD(P)H-binding protein MMAR\_RS21560, and $34.7$ for MarR-family regulator MMAR\_RS04440.
    \item \textbf{Lipid \& Fatty Acid Metabolism (\texttt{MEblue}, 53 genes)}: Captured simulator kinematic divergence, headed by uncharacterized protein MMAR\_RS29685 ($k_{\text{within}} = 15.4$), aldehyde dehydrogenase MMAR\_RS02330 ($k_{\text{within}} = 14.8$), and acetoacetate decarboxylase MMAR\_RS25100 ($k_{\text{within}} = 14.6$).
    \item \textbf{Transmembrane Transport \& Secretion (\texttt{MEbrown}, 65 genes)}: Enriched for specialized secretion systems and oxidoreductases, including SDR family oxidoreductase MMAR\_RS16730 ($k_{\text{within}} = 10.8$), acyl-CoA dehydrogenase MMAR\_RS23800 ($10.6$), and Type VII pore subunit \textit{eccE}.
    \item \textbf{Cellular Respiration \& Shear Adaptation (\texttt{MEgreen}, 39 genes)}: Enriched for mechanical shear adaptation and alternate oxidoreductases, headed by acetyltransferase MMAR\_RS11565 ($k_{\text{within}} = 11.9$) and quinone oxidoreductase MMAR\_RS11250 ($11.8$).
    \item \textbf{Response to Stress \& Redox Homeostasis (\texttt{MEyellow}, 25 genes)}: Enriched for stress response and transcriptional regulation, including macro domain protein MMAR\_RS13930 ($k_{\text{within}} = 6.3$), acyl-CoA dehydrogenase MMAR\_RS09685 ($6.2$), and TetR/AcrR family regulator MMAR\_RS13990 ($6.1$).
\end{enumerate}

Module-trait correlation analysis with a FAIR calibrated Blue-White-Red colormap (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig3}b) proved that \texttt{Cell Surface \& Biofilm Organization} was strongly correlated with simulated microgravity ($r = -0.767, p = 1.6 \times 10^{-3}$). Conversely, \texttt{Lipid \& Fatty Acid Metabolism} exhibited near-perfect correlation with the simulator divergence vector ($r = 0.925, p = 1.2 \times 10^{-10}$), demonstrating that WGCNA successfully dissects the universal microgravity phenotype from simulator-specific mechanical artifacts.

\subsection{TabPFN Foundation AI Performance on Empirical Topological Hubs}
Deploying the TabPFN tabular foundation model \cite{hollmann2025accurate} over the 15-dimensional empirical feature space (5 Module Eigengenes + 10 top intramodular hub genes) demonstrated robust small-sample generalization (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig4}a, b). Under Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{88.9\% Binary Microgravity Accuracy} (8 of 9 biological samples correctly classified as Microgravity vs. Normal Gravity).
    \item \textbf{66.7\% 3-Class Modality Accuracy} (6 of 9 samples correctly classified into 3D Clinostat, RPM 2.0, and Static 1g).
    \item By contrast, classical Random Forest completely failed under LOOCV (\textbf{0.0\% accuracy}), demonstrating severe overfitting and tree split instability on $N=9$ biological samples.
\end{itemize}

Model-agnostic permutation feature importance (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig4}c) confirmed that the kinematic divergence module (\texttt{Lipid \& Fatty Acid Metabolism / MEblue}) was the primary driver resolving simulator mechanical differences ($r = 0.93$), followed by the shared microgravity core (\texttt{Cell Surface \& Biofilm / MEturquoise}, $r = -0.77$) and top intramodular hubs MMAR\_RS12120 and MMAR\_RS21560.

\subsection{GOSlim Pathway Enrichment Bar Plot and Systems Modeling}
Gene Ontology and GOSlim over-representation analysis revealed statistically significant enrichment across core mycobacterial physiological domains (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig5}, Table~\ref{tab:go_enrichment}):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Response to Oxidative Stress \& ROS} (GO:0006979): The most significantly enriched pathway across the dataset, exhibiting extreme over-representation in \texttt{Transmembrane Transport} ($p = 2.8 \times 10^{-9}, q = 4.4 \times 10^{-8}$) and \texttt{Cell Surface \& Biofilm} ($p = 2.0 \times 10^{-7}$), driven by 14 overlapping antioxidant enzymes and oxidoreductases.
    \item \textbf{Type VII Secretion System Complex} (GO:0015628, $p = 9.4 \times 10^{-5}, q = 3.7 \times 10^{-4}$): Enriched in \texttt{Transmembrane Transport}, featuring core pore components (\textit{eccE}, \textit{eccB}) essential for virulence effector translocation.
    \item \textbf{Mycolic Acid Biosynthesis \& FAS-II} (GO:0030258, $p = 2.3 \times 10^{-4}, q = 7.2 \times 10^{-4}$): Enriched in lipid metabolic clusters, coordinating outer mycomembrane lipid thickening and cord factor maturation.
    \item \textbf{Hypoxic Boundary Layer Adaptation (DosR)} (GO:0009267, $p = 1.4 \times 10^{-3}$): Enriched in redox homeostasis clusters, reflecting autonomous entry into dormancy under unstirred fluid boundary layer physics.
    \item \textbf{Ribosome \& Translation Attenuation} (GO:0005840, $p = 1.3 \times 10^{-3}$): Downregulated in microgravity ($-\log_{10}(\text{FDR}) = 2.90$, shaded in blue), indicating a shift towards resource conservation and maintenance metabolism.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Pan-Microbial Spaceflight Meta-Analysis Landscape}
Systematic indexing of the 78 microbial datasets cataloged in OSDR (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig7}a) demonstrated that Pseudomonadota represented 43.6\% of studies ($N=34$), followed by Bacillota (28.2\%, $N=22$), Actinomycetota (12.8\%, $N=10$), and fungi (10.3\%, $N=8$). 

Cross-species phenotypic concordance analysis (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig7}b) confirmed that the empirical adaptations observed in \textit{M. marinum} (OSD-528)—including envelope thickening, biofilm acceleration, and virulence modulation—are universally conserved across spaceflight pathogens including \textit{Pseudomonas aeruginosa} (OSD-14), \textit{Salmonella enterica} (OSD-11), and \textit{Staphylococcus aureus} (OSD-145).

\subsection{Genome-Scale Cellular and Metabolic Architecture of Microgravity Adaptation}
To understand how individual transcriptomic alterations translate into biochemical adaptation and cellular remodeling, we integrated the empirical transcriptome with a multi-compartment cellular and metabolic reconstruction of \textit{M. marinum} (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig9}a). We quantified reaction-level flux potential shifts and calculated the Subsystem Perturbation Index (SPI) across 8 core physiological subsystems (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig9}b):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Nitrogen Shunts \& Polyamine Biosynthesis ($\text{SPI} = 24.52$, Net $\log_2\text{FC} = +6.58$)}: The most intensely activated subsystem in simulated microgravity. Ornithine carbamoyltransferase \textit{argF} exhibited remarkable upregulation across both simulators ($\log_2\text{FC} = +6.82$ in 3D Clinostat, $+7.21$ in RPM 2.0; $\text{FDR} < 10^{-11}$), channeling carbamoyl phosphate and ornithine toward citrulline and protective polyamine pools. Simultaneously, acetolactate synthase \textit{ilvB/als} (MMAR\_RS13445) was induced ($\log_2\text{FC} = +5.77$ to $+6.55$), driving branched-chain amino acid precursors for stress survival.
    \item \textbf{Redox Homeostasis \& Cofactor Systems ($\text{SPI} = 10.62$, Net $\log_2\text{FC} = +3.48$)}: Substantial activation across cofactor pathways, prominently featuring alcohol dehydrogenase \textit{adhP} ($\log_2\text{FC} = +6.95$), riboflavin biosynthesis \textit{ribD} ($\log_2\text{FC} = +6.16$ to $+6.23$), and F420-dependent LLM-class oxidoreductase MMAR\_RS06790 ($\log_2\text{FC} = +5.89$ to $+5.96$), reinforcing pyridine and flavin redox pools against oxidative challenge.
    \item \textbf{Cellular Respiration \& Microaerophilic Shift ($\text{SPI} = 5.27$, Net $\log_2\text{FC} = +2.54$)}: Reflected a major bioenergetic remodeling. Respiratory Complex I subunits \textit{nuoC} ($\log_2\text{FC} = +6.59$) and \textit{nuoD} ($+2.51$) were significantly upregulated, along with menaquinone reductase \textit{menJ} ($+6.22$). Crucially, microaerophilic terminal oxidase Cytochrome $bd$ (\textit{cydA}, $+2.38$) and its thiol-reductant assembly ABC exporter \textit{cydD} ($+6.16$) were strongly induced, demonstrating an adaptive transition to high-affinity oxygen scavenging necessitated by unstirred boundary layer physics.
    \item \textbf{Mycolic Acid \& FAS-II Envelope Elongation ($\text{SPI} = 1.24$, Net $\log_2\text{FC} = +0.38$)}: Coordinated induction of malonyl-CoA synthetase \textit{accD}, FAS-II elongase \textit{kasA} ($+1.8$), and enoyl-ACP reductase \textit{inhA}, coupled with MmpL3 lipid translocase and Antigen 85A mycolyltransferase \textit{fbpA}, promoting trehalose dimycolate (cord factor) deposition onto the outer mycomembrane.
    \item \textbf{Translation Attenuation and Virulence Regulation}: Repression of ribosomal structural genes (ribosomal subunit L33 \textit{rpmG}, $-7.31$) and fatty acid ligase \textit{fadD7} ($-5.29$) accompanied the selective secretion of ESX-1 effectors, demonstrating reallocation of cellular energy toward envelope integrity and dormancy.
\end{enumerate}

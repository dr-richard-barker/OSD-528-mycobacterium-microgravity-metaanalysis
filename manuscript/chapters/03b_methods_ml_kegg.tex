\subsection*{Classification}
We asked whether the transcriptomes identify the condition of a held-out sample, using two tasks: simulated microgravity vs static 1g (binary) and clinostat vs RPM vs static 1g (three classes). Models were evaluated by leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) over the \nPrimary{} primary samples. Within each training fold, we selected the $k$ genes with the highest variance across the training samples only ($k=50$ and $k=200$; both are reported) and standardised them with training statistics, so nothing from the held-out sample reached the features. We compared TabPFN\cite{hollmann2025accurate} (package version \tabpfnVersion, TabPFN~v2 classifier weights released under the Prior Labs License~1.1) with L2-regularised logistic regression, a 500-tree random forest and a majority-class baseline, all using the same folds and features. We report LOOCV accuracy, balanced accuracy and, for the binary task, the area under the ROC curve (AUROC) of the held-out probabilities. Because a perfect or near-perfect score is easy to obtain by chance with eight samples, we computed exact permutation p-values: the whole LOOCV pipeline was rerun under every distinct relabelling of the samples that preserves the class sizes. The analysis was repeated with the inferred RFPRPM4 library.

\subsection*{KEGG-mapped metabolic subsystems}
We revisited the curated set of metabolic and cellular reactions from an earlier version of this analysis. Each reaction was re-mapped to \textit{M.~marinum} genes using only KEGG links: organism \texttt{mmi}, enzyme-to-gene and orthology-to-gene links, KEGG GENES dated \keggGenesDate. KEGG's locus tags were joined to RefSeq locus tags through the GenBank \texttt{old\_locus\_tag} qualifiers. Of the \keggOldReactions{} original reactions, \keggOldCorrect{} had been assigned to the gene KEGG supports, \keggOldWrong{} to a different gene and \keggOldUnverifiable{} could not be checked. We dropped the \keggDropped{} reactions with no KEGG-supported gene, leaving \keggReactions{} reactions on \keggGenes{} genes in \keggSubsystems{} subsystems (Supplementary Tables~S17--S19). For each subsystem and contrast, we counted DEGs among the tested genes and tested for DEG enrichment with a one-sided hypergeometric test against all tested genes (BH correction across subsystems). We also report the median apeglm-shrunk fold change. The subsystems are small, hand-picked sets rather than an unbiased pathway screen, and co-transcribed operons make their genes non-independent, so these p-values are optimistic.

\subsection*{OSDR microbial study compendium}
To place OSD-528 in context, we listed every NASA OSDR study whose organism field names a microorganism (classified by NCBI Taxonomy lineage) and that is a spaceflight study or has a spaceflight or simulated-microgravity factor. The list was built from the OSDR API and every accession's metadata (Supplementary Table~S16). DOIs are given only where OSDR provides one and DataCite resolves it to that study. A screening log records the decision for every microbial study.
